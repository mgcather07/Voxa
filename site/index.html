Read-only by design

The one property that gets it approved.

Every CUCM interaction is a read — AXL SQL queries, RisPort registration lookups, the phones' own web pages. Nothing is ever written back. That's why your voice team can point Voxa at a production cluster, and the log proves it.

10:42:07 AXL executeSQLQuery device, typemodel → 1,622 rows · READ
10:42:09 RIS selectCmDeviceExt registration state → 1,558 up · READ
10:42:11 WEB DeviceInformationX serial, CDP neighbour → READ
10:42:11 AXL update / insert / delete — never issued

On your terms, on your network

Deployed on your VM in an afternoon.

Voxa runs on-prem — a single Docker stack behind your own TLS. It never leaves your network, so it can reach CUCM, the phones, and your switches directly.

1

Install

Load the image, run ./install.sh. It generates secrets, drops in TLS, and starts the stack. A browser wizard creates your admin.

2

Connect

Point Voxa at your publisher with a read-only service account. One connection covers the whole cluster; subscribers are discovered.

3

Collect

Voxa reads configuration, live registration, and each phone's web page — no agents, no writes. CDR flows in on a schedule from your billing server.
